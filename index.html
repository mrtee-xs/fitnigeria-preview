Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

What occurs during the initial consultation?

Your initial appointment consists of an InBody biometric scan, a 7-point functional movement assessment to evaluate joint mobility and muscle imbalances, followed by a goal-setting roadmap with Coach Obinna.

Do you accommodate beginners?

Yes. Every movement is regressed or progressed to match your current biomechanical competency. We prioritize movement mechanics and injury prevention before adding heavy resistance.

How does In-Home Concierge training work?

Our coach travels directly to your residence in Lekki Phase 1, Ikoyi, Banana Island, or Victoria Island with all necessary equipment (kettlebells, resistance bands, suspension trainers) for private coaching on your schedule.
